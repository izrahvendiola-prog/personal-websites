My story

Once upon a "what if..."

My story starts the way most curious stories do: with a question. Somewhere between my first "Hello, World!" and my first wobbly webpage, I realized that making things is the best way I know to understand them — and that sharing the messy middle is half the fun.

Since then I've been collecting skills the way some people collect stickers: a bit of design here, a bit of code there, a growing pile of half-tested ideas in between. I learn in public, build small, and finish most of what I start (the rest become lessons, which also counts).

This website is my semi-portfolio and my playground at once — a place to keep the things I make, the things I'm learning, and the person I'm slowly becoming. If any of that resonates, we'll probably get along great.

— izrah
